FINDINGS SUMMARY
================================================================================

1. Are candidates in the prompts?
   See 'Prompts with at least 1 candidate ID' above.
   For exp19 (BaseLLM): NO candidates — expected (no RAG retrieval).
   For exp22/25/27 (RAG): candidates ARE in prompts (10 per sentence).

2. Is the parser wrong?
   The regex r'\b(T\d{4}(?:\.\d{3})?)\b' correctly extracts all Txxxx patterns.
   This includes codes that appear in model explanations like
   'T1190 : Exfiltration Over Alternative Network Channel'.
   The PARSER IS NOT WRONG — it faithfully extracts what the model wrote.

3. Why are unlisted predictions so high?
   CONFIRMED: The model (llama3.1:8b-instruct-q4_0) freely generates technique
   IDs from its parametric knowledge, ignoring the 'select from the list below'
   instruction. It predicts codes outside the 10 presented candidates, and
   sometimes codes outside the 50-class scope entirely.
   This is model behavior, NOT a parser bug.
   Conclusion: DO NOT change the parser. The unlisted count is a valid signal.

4. Paper context:
   The paper (Schuurman 2025) observes that 8B models have high unlisted rates.
   The parser must EXCLUDE unlisted codes from precision denominator (as implemented).
   Unlisted counts are reported separately in scoring output.
